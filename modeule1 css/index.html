Community Bulletin

The annual city marathon is scheduled for next month. All residents are encouraged to participate. Registration is open at City Hall. The route will pass through the main park and down the riverside. Refreshments will be provided at every 5km mark. Volunteers are needed to help with crowd management. Contact us for more details on how to get involved. The event promises to be bigger and better than ever this year. Local businesses have come forward to sponsor hydration stations along the route. We thank everyone for their continued community spirit and participation.
